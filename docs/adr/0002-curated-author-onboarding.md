# Author profiles are curated for v1, not self-serve

The original idea was open, B2C self-service signup for Authors. We're deferring that: Author profiles represent real public figures' names and brands, so open signup at launch means impersonation risk from day one (anyone could register as a well-known author). For v1, the author manually onboards a handful of real authors he knows. Self-serve signup, with whatever verification that requires, is deferred to a later version once the core follow/update loop is proven.
