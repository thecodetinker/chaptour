# Chaptour

A website where authors have public profiles that readers follow for book-related updates — announcements, tour dates, cover reveals, merch, and buy links — without the social-media mechanics (feeds, likes, comments, engagement notifications) authors would otherwise have to manage. Profiles are fully public: anyone can browse a Bibliography, Upcoming Dates, feed, or Merch without an account. Only following (to receive email) requires anything from a visitor.

## Language

**Author**:
A published or publishing writer with a public profile on Chaptour where they post Updates.
_Avoid_: creator, user (when referring to the author side)

**Follower**:
A person who subscribes to an Author's profile and receives a single email when that Author posts an Update. No feed, no likes, no in-app inbox, no push notifications.
_Avoid_: subscriber, fan

**Book**:
A title by an Author. Persistent — unlike an Update, a Book stays on the Author's profile indefinitely (e.g. in a Bibliography), not just while recent.

**Update**:
A single piece of content an Author posts to their profile, which emails all Followers. Every Update is one of: Announcement, Tour Date, or Cover Reveal. Announcements and Tour Dates are ephemeral: visible on the main profile for 90 days from the relevant date, then archived to Past Updates — never deleted, just off the main page. Cover Reveal is the exception: it's ephemeral as a feed post, but it also sets the cover art on a Book, which persists.

**Announcement**:
A general-news Update from an Author with no fixed structure (e.g. "signed a new book deal"). Ephemeral; archives 90 days after posting.

**Tour Date**:
An Update describing a scheduled public appearance — date, location, and optionally a link. Ephemeral; archives 90 days after the date passes.

**Cover Reveal**:
An Update that sets or replaces the cover art on one of an Author's Books.

**Past Updates**:
The archive an Announcement or Tour Date moves to once its visibility window elapses. Still discoverable, just off the main profile.

**Buy Link**:
A persistent link on a Book pointing to an external retailer where it can be purchased. Editable anytime by the Author; does not post to the feed or email Followers.
_Avoid_: purchase link

**Merch Link**:
A persistent link in an Author's Merch section pointing to an external store selling their merchandise. Editable anytime by the Author; does not post to the feed or email Followers.
